# LENS Battery Working Group – 12/11/26

## Summary

The discussion focused on two key topics:

1. **Standardisation of battery experiments, cells and data**
2. **Autonomous battery experiments**

### 1. Standardisation of Battery Experiments, Cells and Data

There was broad agreement on the value of improving standardisation and metadata recording across facilities, particularly in the following areas:

- **Battery cells:** Developing guidelines for cell design, fabrication and electrode preparation, recognising that requirements may differ depending on the experiment.
- **Experimental conditions:** Ensuring consistent recording of parameters such as stack pressure, temperature and electrochemical cycling protocols.
- **Data and metadata:** Agreeing on what information should be collected and how it should be stored to enable comparison and reuse across facilities.

The emphasis should be on improving consistency and transparency, rather than requiring users to adopt identical cells, measurement procedures or data architectures.

### 2. Autonomous Battery Experiments

Automation was of interest to different facilities for two main reasons:

- Reducing instrument scientist input and time.
- Making more efficient use of beamtime.

However, there was no agreement on a potential use case for automation that would be instrument-agnostic.

Several AI-related topics were also discussed:

- **AI-assisted experiment planning:** Including cell design, diffraction simulations and optimisation of measurement parameters.
- **Automated data acquisition:**
  - Introducing cut-offs when sufficient signal-to-noise has been achieved.
  - Triggering more frequent measurements near phase transitions or when the sample is changing more rapidly.
